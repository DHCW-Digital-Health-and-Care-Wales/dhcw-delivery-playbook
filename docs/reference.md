# Reference and further reading

## Digital Service Standard for Wales

All DHCW teams delivering public-facing services are assessed against the Digital Service Standard for Wales, published by the Centre for Digital Public Services (CDPS). The standard covers user research, accessibility, Welsh language, technology choices, and agile ways of working.

[cdps.wales](https://cdps.wales) – Digital Service Standard for Wales

## DDaT Capability Framework

Role titles, competencies, and career pathways for all digital, data, and technology roles in UK government and devolved public bodies. The DDaT framework is the basis for all DHCW job evaluation in product and service delivery.

[ddat.tech.gov.uk](https://ddat.tech.gov.uk)

## The 14 Continuous Delivery Markers

DHCW's benchmark for engineering practice, based on the continuous delivery research of Jez Humble and Dave Farley and adapted for the DHCW context. Available in the DHCW Engineering Handbook.

## DHCW AI-Infused Engineering Operating Model

The organisational strategy for responsible adoption of AI tooling in software engineering. Sets out the sequencing logic: engineering foundations first, AI tooling second.

## DHCW Solution Architecture Strategy

The architectural principles governing all DHCW product decisions: portable by default, open standards over proprietary APIs, Kubernetes as the deployment target.
